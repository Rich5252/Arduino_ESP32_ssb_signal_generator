Extending ampeq toward 8000Hz: projected delay cost

Two measured curves (real TFA sweeps, same as gdeq_ampeq_delay_chart_v4.html) plus a third, NOT-yet-measured, projected curve: the real 'g'+'a' sweep with shelf2's own analytic group delay (fc=6000Hz, +10dB) added on top by superposition. This is a prediction of what a real 'g'+'a'+shelf2 sweep would show if gdeq is NOT refit first — it has not been built or bench-tested.

This is a "before you refit gdeq" warning, not a measurement

Shelf2 was designed 2026-09-04 to claw back more of the measured 8000Hz insertion loss (net -15.70dB → -5.70dB). Its own analytic group delay is larger than shelf1's (125.4 µs p-p vs. 64.9 µs) — not a flaw in this particular shelf, but an unavoidable consequence of a causal minimum-phase magnitude filter: more high-frequency gain recovered means more nearby phase/delay distortion, for any shelf shape. gdeq has never been refit against shelf1 OR shelf2 — its coefficients still only know about the bare analog filter. Projected onto the real 'g'+'a' measurement, adding shelf2 on top of an unrefit gdeq would push dispersion from 81.4 µs p-p to roughly 167 µs p-p — worse than having no group-delay equalizer at all was ever measured to be.

What this does and doesn't say

It is not a reason to abandon shelf2. The magnitude side is the whole point of extending ampeq, and the 3100–8000Hz net-correction improvement is real and substantial on paper (not yet bench-confirmed either). What this projection says is narrower: enabling shelf2 without also refitting gdeq would very likely make the group-delay side worse than it's ever been measured in this project, including worse than having 'g' off entirely at some frequencies — so a two-tone IMD test run in that state would be conflating "more amplitude correction" with "much more delay dispersion," and a worse (or better) IMD result wouldn't cleanly tell you which one caused it.

Recommended order: (1) flash shelf1+shelf2, run the 'w' chirp/TFA sweep to get the REAL combined magnitude+phase response (this chart's dashed line is only a superposition estimate, not a substitute for that); (2) refit gdeq's two all-pass coefficients against that new combined phase curve, same method as its last two refits (see envelope_gdeq.h's history); (3) only then run the two-tone IMD comparison, so any IMD change reflects the amplitude correction on its own, with group delay held flat the way it's been for every other test in this project.
