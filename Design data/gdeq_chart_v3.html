Reading this result

Over 100–8000 Hz: analog alone 71.3 µs p-p (essentially identical to the previous session's 71.2 µs — the Hi-Z buffers are giving repeatable measurements) → with the new coefficients, 20.3 µs p-p, a real ~3.5× reduction. Mean added delay measured at 125.2 µs (2.003 samples @ 16 kHz) — within 1 µs of the fit's own 125.4 µs/2.006-sample prediction. The predicted curve tracks the real measured "on" curve to within ~2 µs RMS (4.4 µs worst-case) across the whole band — the numeric fit is behaving on the bench essentially the way the model said it would.

What this validates, and what it doesn't: the group-delay/dispersion side of the refit is now confirmed on real hardware, not just predicted. It does not by itself confirm the equalizer helps IMD3 — the earlier real-hardware finding that the old coefficients hurt IMD3 was driven by factors an all-pass delay fit can't touch (insertion loss, a possible 'I' interpolation confound), and those haven't been re-measured with these coefficients. g stays off by default until that IMD delay-sweep is re-run.
