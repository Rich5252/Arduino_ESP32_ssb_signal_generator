Reading this result

Over the design band the equalizer adds ~40 µs mean delay (predicted ~132 µs) and leaves peak-to-peak dispersion essentially unchanged (146→147 µs, vs. a predicted 41→14 µs). The 3000–4300 Hz sub-band dominates that overall figure and shows no improvement; 100–1000 Hz shows a real ~28% reduction (29→21 µs) and 2000–3000 Hz a smaller one (69→67 µs). Residual phase noise (raw minus smoothed) rises from ~0.4° at 100–1000 Hz to ~2.5° near 3000–4300 Hz, close to the filter’s own corner — a plausible confound that could be masking real correction at the top of the band, not proof the equalizer is working there. No magnitude/coherence channel was captured, so that can’t be fully disentangled from this dataset alone.
