This is a clean, working linearization. From about frac 0.02 (right at the turn-on knee, just above the analog noise floor) all the way to full scale, measured output tracks the ideal proportional-amplitude line to within ±0.5 dB, with an RMS error under 0.15 dB across the whole usable range — no systematic bow or drift in either direction, just small, evenly-mixed ripple consistent with real measurement noise. This is the first real-hardware confirmation that envelope_predistort_process() ('D') actually linearizes envelope→RF-amplitude on this board, not just against its own fitting data.

This run only has predistort ON — it confirms the corrected mapping is good, but doesn't by itself show how much worse the raw ('D' off) mapping would have been. The companion pass with 'E' still on but 'D' off would show that directly, and is the other half of the originally-planned A/B.
